## About me

I am a third-year undergraduate at the **Faculty of Information Technology, University of Moratuwa**, and a software developer who enjoys turning ideas into dependable, user-focused software.

- Building full-stack products, backend services, mobile experiences, and AI-assisted tools
- Exploring system design, machine learning, developer tooling, and product engineering
- Learning in public through experiments, open-source collaboration, and iterative delivery
